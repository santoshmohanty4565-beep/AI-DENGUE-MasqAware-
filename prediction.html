Golden Rules to Stay Safe
Essential Community & Individual Guidelines to Eliminate Vector Breeding & Protect Families
ODISHA PUBLIC HEALTH PROTOCOL
💧
Rule #1
Remove Standing Water
🗓️ Perform Action Every Week:
  • Empty buckets, flower pots, coolers, tyres, bottles, and coconut shells.
  • Cover all water storage containers tightly with lids or mesh screens.
🦟
Rule #2
Protect Yourself from Bites
🛡️ Personal Shield Measures:
  • Wear full-sleeved, light-colored clothes to cover skin.
  • Use mosquito repellent (DEET, Picaridin, or Lemon Eucalyptus).
  • Sleep under mosquito nets (especially children & elderly).
🏠
Rule #3
Mosquito-Proof Your Home
🚪 Household Defenses:
  • Install window and door screens (mesh wire).
  • Keep surroundings clean and free of dark corners.
  • Use mosquito traps or indoor insecticides responsibly.
⏰
Rule #4 • Critical Vector Timing
Aedes Mosquitoes Bite During the Day!
Be extra vigilant during peak biting activity windows:
🌅 Early Morning: 6:00 AM – 9:00 AM 🌆 Late Afternoon: 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM
🧹
Rule #5 • Civic Action
Maintain Community Cleanliness
  • Join neighborhood clean-up drives to eliminate communal larvae hotspots.
  • Report blocked drains, broken pipes, or stagnant water to local municipal authorities (BMC/CMC/104 Helpline).
