Key Epidemiological Insights
🌡️
Temperature Lag Effect
Temperature at lag 1–5 months has 41% contribution to outbreak risk. >27°C triggers accelerated Aedes breeding cycles.
🌧️
Monsoon Paradox
Moderate rainfall (50–150mm) increases breeding sites. Heavy rainfall (>200mm) flushes larvae, temporarily reducing risk.
🧬
DENV-2 Dominance
67% of isolates are DENV-2, associated with DHF. 13/18 districts confirmed DENV-2 circulation. Secondary infection risk elevated.
📉
Intervention Success
2025's 73% case reduction is attributed to pre-monsoon fogging, weekly dry-day drives, and community-level breeding site elimination.
🏙️
Urban Hotspot Pattern
Bhubaneswar micro-pockets (Patia, Sikharchandi, Chandrasekharpur) contribute 35% of Khordha district's total burden. Construction site water accumulation is key driver.
🏠
Housing Risk Factor
Thatched housing has OR 3.0 for dengue infection vs concrete structures. Occupational travel (OR 3.0) and peridomestic water storage (OR 1.7) are significant cofactors.
